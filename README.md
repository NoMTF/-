# The Life of Richard Scolyer

**[The_Life_of_Richard_Scolyer.mp4](The_Life_of_Richard_Scolyer.mp4)**: a 4 min 52 s animated biography (1920×1080, 25 fps, stereo AAC) made in the style of *The Life of Fred Hollows*. It has an orange collage look, chapter title cards, a cartoon "cut-out" host character, stat graphics, and red theatre curtains at the start and end.

Professor Richard Scolyer AO (1966–2026) was a melanoma pathologist and co-medical director of Melanoma Institute Australia. He was joint 2024 Australian of the Year with Professor Georgina Long, and the world's first patient to receive immunotherapy before surgery for glioblastoma.

## How it was made
I made everything for this film: the script, the illustrations and animation (HTML/SVG rendered frame by frame in headless Chromium), and the music (composed as MIDI and rendered with the FluidR3 GM soundfont). The narration uses a synthetic voice (Kokoro TTS). No real photos or footage of Richard Scolyer are used, because none are freely licensed.

The facts come from ABC News (8 June 2026), Melanoma Institute Australia / The University of Sydney (tribute, 13 July 2026) and Wikipedia. The quotes on screen are Richard Scolyer's own words as the ABC reported them. The map outlines come from Natural Earth (public domain). The fonts are Caprasimo, Open Sans, Nunito and Caveat (SIL OFL).

The source is in [`film/`](film). To rebuild the video, run `film/setup.sh` once, then `film/build.sh`.
